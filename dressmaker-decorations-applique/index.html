Dressmaker Decorations, Trim & Appliqué

After panels are sewn, Dressmaker lets you finish garments with trim, lace, buttons, bows, appliqué, and accessories. This page covers the finishing step for the Steam game and how heavy decoration runs feed certain achievements.

TL;DR — Decorate after construction; watch budget

  1. Finish cut + sew first.
  2. Add decorations that fit the client’s occasion and remaining budget.
  3. Don’t confuse decoration spam with grain/sewing quality.
  4. For achievement dumps (50m+ trim, 300+ accessories), use a dedicated dress and sell if required See Achievements.

FAQ

When do I decorate?
After construction; before final delivery/sale.

Do decorations fix a bad grain cut?
No. Fix grain/sewing first.

Must I sell for the accessory achievement?
Secondary guides say yes — confirm in Steam.

Can shop-window dresses use the same tools?
Expect the same finishing tools; confirm any shop-window quirks on your build.

Where is the full achievement list?
See Achievements.
