About This Dressmaker Guide

This site publishes English how-to guides for Dressmaker on Steam (developed by Cozy Lives, published by Free Lives). We focus on playable systems: the commission loop, grain-line cutting, sewing quality, fabrics & style tags, pattern layout, decorations, and achievements.

What we cover

  • Decision trees and checklists (“do this before you cut”)
  • Tables for troubleshooting quality, tags, and missable achievements
  • Internal links between related systems

What we don’t try to be

  • A complete fabric SKU database
  • An official patch channel
  • A walkthrough that spoils every client beat in novel form

Independence

This is an unofficial fan guide site. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Free Lives, Cozy Lives, or Valve. Game names and assets belong to their owners.

How guides stay current

When Steam patches change controls or scoring, we revise the affected pages and update the “checked as of” line near the top. Prefer fewer precise claims over outdated numbers.
