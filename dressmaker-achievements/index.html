Dressmaker Achievements Guide: All 32, Hidden & Missable

Dressmaker ships with 32 Steam achievements, and 16 of them are hidden on Steam. Most unlock through normal play: finishing each client’s major commissions (see Commissions & Clients) and completing event dress sets. A few are easy to miss (dialogue, pigeon track, sell-after-crafting), and the 100% quality unlock rewards careful pattern piece layout plus accurate stitching at the sewing machine.

Hidden means Steam keeps the unlock description secret until you earn it (16 of 32). Missable: Yes is a one-time trigger you can lose; Easy to miss is a condition players often fail on a given dress; — means not flagged as missable. Details for each group are in the tables below.

TL;DR — Missables first

  1. Watch Vivian’s animal dialogue → choose Leech for the related achievement.
  2. Finish all pigeon orders (separate track).
  3. For heavy accessory/trim challenges, sell the dress if the UI requires it.
  4. Complete each client chain and each event set fully.
  5. Craft challenges: first Ruffle Skirt, 100% quality, big trim, big accessory count, high spend success.

Suggested cleanup order (low spoiler)

  1. Enable a personal checklist for missables (Leech, pigeon, Dorothy first dress).
  2. Keep client chains tidy while playing naturally.
  3. Sweep event sets before moving on when an occasion appears.
  4. Park expensive craft achievements for late economy.

FAQ

How many achievements?
32 on Steam: 10 client chains, 14 event dress sets, 5 crafting and spending challenges, and 3 story, dialogue and pigeon unlocks.

Which achievements are hidden?
16 of the 32. Steam hides the descriptions of all 14 event dress set achievements plus You’ve Got Mail and Sucker for Punishment until you unlock them. The table at the top lists every one.

What can I permanently miss?
Sucker for Punishment (pick Leech in Vivian’s animal dialogue), Birds of a Feather, Frock Together (finish every pigeon order) and You’ve Got Mail (early Dorothy beat) are the usual horror stories — handle those early.

Do event sets need perfect scores?
Secondary guides say completion of the full set is enough — perfect scores are usually not required.

Does assist mode block 100% quality?
Check your Steam unlock requirements and Settings assist notes before grinding 100% with assist on.

Must accessory dresses be sold?
Guides say yes for Harder than it Seams — sell the loaded dress if the unlock does not pop.
