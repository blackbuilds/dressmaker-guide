Dressmaker How to Play: Beginner Guide

This page teaches the Dressmaker commission loop on Steam so your first deliveries succeed. It is a priority guide with “done when” checkboxes — not a full client encyclopedia.

Disambiguation: This is the Free Lives / Cozy Lives game, not real-world dressmaking books or sewing-machine manuals.

TL;DR — Commission loop order

  1. Read the client brief (style targets + budget + occasion).
  2. Take measurements / fit inputs.
  3. Design in the sketchbook; preview style tags with fabric samples before buying → Fabrics & Style Tags.
  4. Buy fabric within budget; assign panels thoughtfully → Pattern Pieces.
  5. Align grain arrows until you see the green outline, then cut → Grain Line & Cutting.
  6. Sew on the guideline for accuracy → Sewing Quality.
  7. Decorate (trim / appliqué / accessories) → Decorations.
  8. Deliver; watch client reaction / payout.

Ignore on day 1: sabotage runs, 300-accessory grinds, max-trim dumps, chasing every event set.

What Dressmaker is

Dressmaker is a cozy crafting sim: you design dresses, cut patterns on fabric, sew panels, decorate, and satisfy (or later, sabotage) townsfolk. Steam lists single-player, achievements, and Cloud support. Core materials called out on the store page include cotton, linen, wool, silk, and velvet (examples — not an exhaustive catalog). Every dress runs through the same three checkpoints: lay out your pattern pieces, stitch them cleanly at the sewing machine, and deliver what the client asked for — the commissions and clients guide explains budgets and client chains.

FAQ

Is this the Steam full release or the old itch prototype?
Guides here target the Steam full release (~2026-09-21). Prototype articles can still be useful for concepts but may show outdated controls or UI.

Why is my quality low when sewing looks fine?
Usually grain misalignment at cut time. Confirm green outlines on every piece → Grain.

How do style tags work?
Tags come from fabric + dress pattern together. Drag samples into the sketchbook before you spend → Fabrics.

Should I sabotage clients on day 1?
No. Learn successful deliveries first. Sabotage is a later topic (Steam mentions satisfy or sabotage).

Where are all 32 achievements?
→ Achievements.

What should I open if I’m stuck mid-dress?
